abstract: |
  Subtle, low-effort finger interactions are important for wearable and augmented reality systems, but are difficult to support under tight power and form-factor constraints. We present picoRing, a battery-free ring that enables thumb-to-index input using low-power sensing and energy harvesting. picoRing demonstrates how always-available, self-powered wearable input can be achieved through careful co-design of sensing, hardware, and interaction techniques.
