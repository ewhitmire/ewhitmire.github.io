abstract: |
  Detecting subtle finger motion on the wrist is challenging due to noise, variability in contact, and sensing constraints. We present SoundScroll, a wearable system that uses friction sound captured by wrist-worn microphones to robustly detect finger sliding gestures. The system demonstrates how acoustic sensing can support reliable, low-effort interaction in real-world wearable scenarios.
